# Oryn — The work knows its way back

**Concept.** An invoice travels through a living operational loop: from a person’s permitted work, to a reviewed playbook, to an agent that must prove its outcomes, stop when uncertain, and earn its way back after a change. We start with a human face and the noise of work, then reveal an immense but legible instrument built from orbit rings, documents and evidence. The camera moves through a two-dimensional dotted canvas rather than a three-dimensional world. The product is quiet, precise and flat inside its windows; the surrounding stage supplies scale, kinetic type, hard shadows and cinematic impact. The most arresting moment is not an agent acting, but an agent refusing to guess. Its release after a human-guided repair feels earned, not magical.

**Runtime:** 03:00.00. **Format:** 1280 × 720 CSS px, captured at DPR 2 / 2560 × 1440, delivered 1920 × 1080, 60 fps. **Audience:** enterprise operations leaders, software engineers and hackathon judges. **Speaker:** Harikeshav Rameshkumar alone, in person for the hook and close and as the sole voice throughout.

Second, at 01:37–01:46 and 01:58–02:04, a safe escalation earns a pass and an uncertain ERP submission is held: the soundtrack thins and a neutral hatch makes restraint visible. Third, at 02:17–02:24, the repaired version earns a narrow release, three independently verified outcomes and owner-directed expansion; the orbit closes only on verification, never merely on submission.

### Global continuity and production rules

All coordinates are authored 1280 × 720, origin top left. A black subtitle strip remains present across every entry, including silent entries; when VO is `—`, the strip has no glyphs. It sits at x=128–1152, y=630–688, with one line of white IBM Plex Mono 600 at 13–15px on `#0A0A0A`; exact captions are indexed in the appendix. Keep essential UI and lower-thirds above y=630. Captions follow the VO verbatim and do not duplicate UI descriptions. Unless a shot says otherwise, product interiors use bone `#F6F6F3`, white, ink `#0A0A0A`, warm 1px `#DFDFD8` rules, 8/6/4px radii, flat surfaces and Lucide icons; Space Grotesk headings, IBM Plex Mono labels and Crimson Text human prose. Stage-only cards have 2px ink outlines and 6px offset ink shadows; stage dots are 1px at 24px intervals, opacity .12. No hard shadow enters a product window. Labels are at least 11px authored. Status combines text with distinct glyphs: success check, warning triangle, failed cross, unknown diagonal hatch; cyan is reserved for the action executing at that instant and extinguishes when it stops. Passive observation uses a neutral icon and text, not cyan.

Defaults: product state changes take 150ms `cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1)`; stage reveals and camera moves use `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`; pointer moves follow a cubic path with 1–3% overshoot; click is 90ms scale .97 plus a 260ms ink-alpha ring. All moves and type are deterministic. Type-on runs 28–34 characters/s unless a cut starts with pre-existing content. Caption assignments are editorial segments of the transcript, not additional speech. A slash in a TEXT line separates concurrent elements rather than appearing onscreen.
